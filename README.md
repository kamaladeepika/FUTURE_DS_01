# Mapping the Global Mental Health Crisis — Power BI Dashboard

An interactive dashboard built in **Power BI Desktop** to analyze global mental health indicators across countries, regions, and income groups.

## Objective

To visualize patterns in mental health crisis index, anxiety, depression, suicide rates, psychiatrist availability, and social media usage, and derive meaningful insights through interactive analysis.

## Key KPIs

* Average Mental Health Crisis Index
* Anxiety and Depression Percentage
* Total Affected Population (millions)
* Suicide Rate per 100K
* Psychiatrists per 100K
* Daily Social Media Usage (hours)

## Visual Analysis

* Region and income-group comparison of crisis levels
* Country-wise mental health index distribution
* Psychiatrists availability by country
* Social media usage patterns
* Interactive filtering with slicers (Year, Region, Country, Income Group, MH Law)

## Deeper Insights

* Clear disparity in crisis levels between regions, highlighting uneven global mental health burden.
* Upper-middle and lower-middle income groups show elevated crisis indicators compared to high-income groups.
* Countries with fewer psychiatrists per 100K population tend to show higher crisis and suicide rates.
* Higher daily social media usage aligns with increased anxiety and depression percentages in several countries.
* Regions without strong mental health laws show comparatively worse crisis metrics.
* A small group of countries contributes disproportionately to the total affected population.
* Suicide rate trends closely mirror the mental health crisis index in multiple regions.
* Variations across income groups suggest resource access plays a key role in mental health outcomes.
* Filtering by year reveals changing patterns that indicate rising awareness and reporting in recent periods.

## Tools & Techniques

* Power Query for data cleaning
* DAX measures for KPIs
* Data modeling and relationships
* Insight-driven visual design
